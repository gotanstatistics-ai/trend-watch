The world's oldest known shipwreck has been found: a trader that sank 4,500 years ago

• Found 30 metres down near Kekova Island on Turkey's Mediterranean coast; more than 30 clay storage jars and three stone anchors lie on the seabed.
• Dated to 4,400 to 4,700 years ago from the pottery, since no wood survived. That is older than the pyramids of Giza.
• The clay points to workshops in the Jordan Valley, so this ship was carrying goods across the sea in the Early Bronze Age.
• The previous record holder, off the Greek island of Dokos, dates to about 2200 BC.
